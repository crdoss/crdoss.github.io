Charles R. Doss

Associate Professor and Director of Graduate Studies
School of Statistics, University of Minnesota

I work on nonparametric estimation and inference, in particular for functions satisfying shape constraints, on causal inference, and on empirical process theory and its applications.

I did my Ph.D. at the University of Washington with Jon Wellner, and my B.S. in mathematics at the University of Chicago.
